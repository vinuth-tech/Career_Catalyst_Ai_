CareerCatalyst AI
From Skills to Career Success with AI

CareerCatalyst AI is an AI-powered career mentor for students. You upload your resume (or type your skills), choose a career goal, and the app gives you a full career report in a few seconds: your skill gaps, a 4-month learning roadmap, project ideas, and interview questions.

Built for a hackathon as a solo project.

The Problem
Most students know what they want to become, but they do not know what to learn next. They end up watching random tutorials, building random projects, and still feeling unprepared for interviews.

Our Solution
Upload your resume, pick a role, and get a clear personal plan:

What skills you already have
What skills you are missing (with priority)
What to learn each month
Which projects to build (beginner to advanced)
Which interview questions to practise
Features
Resume upload: upload a PDF resume and the app reads your skills from it automatically
Manual input: no resume? Just type your name, education and skills
Career score and interview readiness: shown with green, yellow and red progress bars
Skill gap detector: missing skills sorted by priority (high, medium, low)
4-month roadmap: a simple month-by-month learning plan
Project recommendations: 6 projects, 2 each for beginner, intermediate and advanced
Interview coach: 10 role-specific questions with short answer hints
Demo fallback: if the API fails, one click loads a sample report
